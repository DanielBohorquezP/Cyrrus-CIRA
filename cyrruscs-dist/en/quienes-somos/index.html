CEO & Founder, Cyrrus Consulting Services

Jackson Bohorquez founded Cyrrus Consulting Services to close the gap he sees causing most organizations to fail: not a lack of strategy or technology, but the fact that no one connects the decision to the execution. That's where the CIRA method was born — Construct, Identify, Realize, Adopt — the framework that now guides Cyrrus's digital transformation projects in more than 17 countries.

"Organizations don't fail for lack of strategy or technology. They fail when no one connects the decision to the execution. CIRA exists to close exactly that gap."
