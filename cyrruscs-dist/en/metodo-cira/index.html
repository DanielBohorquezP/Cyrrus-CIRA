What is the CIRA Method? The full cycle of your transformation

CIRA Method is Cyrrus's proprietary methodology that unifies the four phases of digital transformation — Construct, Identify, Realize, Adopt — under a single team accountable from start to finish, with artificial intelligence accelerating every phase.

Construct the strategy

A strategy gets defined... but no one knows how to bring it down to the operation. Our strategy consulting connects the business vision to an executable plan, not a document that ends up in a drawer. In-depth business diagnosis, prioritization, and an actionable roadmap — delivered as CTO as a Service or CIO as a Service when the organization lacks that capability in-house.

Our ERP and enterprise software selection consulting always starts from the business's actual requirements, not the vendor's catalog. 100% vendor-agnostic technology evaluation, with objective, comparable criteria and no commercial bias toward any brand.

Frequently asked questions

It depends on the organization's scope and size, but as a reference: Construct (strategy) usually takes 4 to 8 weeks, Identify (solution selection) 6 to 12 weeks, Realize (project) varies by the project executed, and Adopt (change) is worked transversally from day one.

Each phase — Strategy, Select, Project Management, and Change Management — is hired independently. Most of our clients start with a single phase and expand scope once they see results.

We evaluate the decision with the same objective criteria, even if the process has already started. We're 100% agnostic: we have no commercial partnerships or commissions with ERP, CRM, HCM, or other enterprise solution manufacturers.

AI is measured with the same governance discipline as the rest of the method — it's not a separate layer. It accelerates tasks within each phase: diagnosis in Construct, parallel vendor evaluation in Identify, real-time risk monitoring in Realize, and continuous adoption measurement in Adopt.
