CIRA Method — Identify

Technology selection without vendor bias

Technology gets bought because it's trending, not because it truly fits the business. Our technology selection always starts from the business's requirements, not the vendor's catalog — 100% agnostic evaluation, with objective, comparable criteria.

How we do it

A solution built for your business context, not the vendor's catalog.

We take a comprehensive approach that covers key factors like your industry, company size, technological maturity, and business processes. Our goal is to offer you a tailored solution that fits your needs and business context perfectly.

  • 360° assessment of your business's sector, size, and technical maturity.
  • 100% agnostic comparison, with no commercial bias toward any brand.
  • Objective, comparable criteria, defined before looking at vendors.

Our challenge

Selecting solutions built to fit your organization.

The challenge for corporations isn't finding vendors — it's making sure the vendor delivers on its promises and fits the operation without friction. We analyze every variable in record time.
